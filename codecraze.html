How It Works

LEVEL 1
Get a Theme
Every team is handed a trendy, unconventional theme at the start — no picking, no planning ahead. That's the fun part.
LEVEL 2
Choose Your Problem Statement
Based on your theme, lock in a problem statement that your team wants to tackle within the time limit.
LEVEL 3
Brainstorm · Build · Present
Race against the clock to ideate, build, and present — all within the 3-hour window. No intense rules, just momentum.
03

Choose Your Quest

Tap a theme to see sample problem statements — these are starting points, not the only options.

  • Rage Meter — Create a system that assigns a Rage Score (0–100) to a piece of online content, based on factors like emotional language, provocation, exaggeration, and engagement-bait patterns.
  • Petty Complaint Box — A platform where students post tiny everyday annoyances — AC not working, canteen out of samosas — and it auto-generates dramatic "official complaints."

  • Broke Till Payday — An expense tracker for students that roasts bad spending habits instead of just showing boring graphs.
  • Fake Rich Calculator — Enter your pocket money and it shows, in funny comparisons, how "rich" or "broke" you really are — e.g. "you can afford 3 momos and emotional damage."

  • Campus Lost & Found Detective — Matches lost-item reports with found-item reports using descriptions, images, locations, or semantic similarity.
  • Club Event Radar — One place showing all club events happening on campus that week, so students stop missing them.
  • Choose Your Own Adventure — A branching interactive story where every decision leads to increasingly chaotic consequences.
  • Hostel Survival Simulator — A game/simulator where students try to survive hostel life.

  • What Should I Do First? — Uses deadlines, importance, effort, and dependencies to turn a chaotic task list into a sensible priority order.
  • AI Excuse Generator — Generates funny, believable excuses for missed deadlines or classes — built as a joke tool, not for real misuse.
  • Superhero LinkedIn — Heroes and villains create profiles, endorsements, achievements, and questionable career histories.
  • Villain Dating App — Match supervillains based on evil compatibility.
